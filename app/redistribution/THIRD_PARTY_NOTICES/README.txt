Hush third-party notices

The separate top-level LICENSE applies to Hush. It does not relicense other
works. INDEX.tsv identifies third-party components, the terms and their sources.
Identical notices share a SHA-256-named file under texts/. A row for an
alternative or a conservative build input does not imply that all of its code
shipped. The index identifies the official Rust standard-library notice that is
stored as HTML text.

Hush bundles and transforms JavaScript and worker modules. It embeds locked
WASM payloads, including the source-built Photon web module and its generated
JavaScript. Notes on the inputs and rebuilds are in app/wasm/README.md at the
release source. These archives do not contain Node.js.

Hush makes no claim to third-party marks or endorsement.
